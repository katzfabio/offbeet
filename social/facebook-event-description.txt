Offbeet is an instrumental quartet here in Ottawa: Jim Gamo on drums, Rob Coplan on keyboards, Dan Sharon on bass and sax, and Fabio Katz on guitar. We play originals alongside covers that wander through rock, jazz, classical, world music, and the occasional TV theme.

HOW TO GET TICKETS
Send an Interac e-transfer of $20 per person to offbeetottawa@gmail.com, and put your name and the number of tickets in the message. Your name goes on the list at the door: nothing to print, no service fees, no ticketing website.

Tickets are advance only — we can't take cash at the door, and once we're sold out that's it, so don't leave it too late.
