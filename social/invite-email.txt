Subject: Offbeet live — Sunday, September 13


Hi everybody,

My band Offbeet is playing a show next month, and I'd love for you to come.

We're an instrumental quartet here in Ottawa: Jim Gamo on drums, Rob Coplan
on keyboards, Dan Sharon on bass and sax, and me on guitar. We play originals
alongside covers that wander through rock, jazz, classical, world music, and
the occasional TV theme. No vocals — just four of us and a lot of enthusiasm.

    Sunday, September 13
    Hummingbird Hall, 900 Greenbank Rd, Ottawa, ON K2J 4P6
    Doors 6:30 PM · Music 7:00–9:00 PM
    $20 per person

How to get tickets: send an Interac e-transfer of $20 per person to
offbeetottawa@gmail.com, and put your name and the number of tickets in the
message. Your name goes on the list at the door — nothing to print, no
service fees, no ticketing website. Tickets are advance only; we can't take
cash at the door, and once we're sold out that's it, so don't leave it too
late.

If you'd like a preview, there are a few videos and the rest of the band at
offbeet.ca.

Hope to see you there,
Fabio
