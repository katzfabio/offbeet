Subject: Offbeet live — Sunday, September 13


Hi —

My band Offbeet is playing a show next month, and I'd love for you to come.

four of us and a lot of enthusiasm.

    Sunday, September 13
    Hummingbird Hall, 900 Greenbank Rd, Ottawa, ON K2J 4P6
    Doors 6:30 PM · Music 7:00–9:00 PM
    $20

How to get tickets: send an Interac e-transfer of $20 per person to
offbeetottawa@gmail.com, and put your name and the number of tickets in
the message. Your name goes on the list at the door — nothing to
print, no service fees, no ticketing website.

If you'd like a preview, there are a few videos and the rest of the band at
offbeet.ca.

Hope to see you there,
Fabio
